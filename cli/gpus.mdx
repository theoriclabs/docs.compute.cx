```bash
compute gpu list
```

That is the catalog you can launch against right now. Rates are cents per hour and can change.

## What we launch today

| You type | Machine | Provider |
|---|---|---|
| `H100-SXM` or `H100` | RunPod Secure H100-SXM | `runpod` |
| `MI300X` | Hot Aisle MI300X | `hotaisle` |

`--gpu H100` and `--gpu H100-80GB` normalize to **H100-SXM**.

## Policies

`compute run --gpu auto|fastest|cheapest` picks from the live catalog. `auto` balances time and cost. You can still pass `--provider runpod` or `--provider hotaisle` to stay on one side.

If the selector would fall back to a different SKU than you asked for, the CLI asks before creating the run. `--allow-selector-fallback` / `--yes` accepts that without a prompt.

## What is not in the public catalog

A100, 4090, Community/spot shapes, and multi-GPU are not a v0.1 launch path. If `gpu list` does not show a SKU, do not assume `compute run` will get it.
